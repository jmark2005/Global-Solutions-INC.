About the System

What is S4MS?

The Smart School Safety and Student Support Management System (S4MS) is a project by Global Solutions INC. designed to centralize and streamline all activities related to student safety incidents and student support services within a designated school.

The system tracks safety incidents, semester attendance, and support services while maintaining clear records of participation by administrators, teachers, counselors, and other school personnel.

By providing a digital dashboard and facility mapping, S4MS enables faster response times and informed decision-making at every level of school management.

Administrators

Full dashboard access to monitor, manage, and respond to all incidents and user accounts.

Teachers & Staff

Submit reports, view assigned tasks, and coordinate support activities efficiently.

Students

Access support resources, trigger emergency alerts, and monitor their own attendance.

Counselors

Track student welfare activities and document support service completion records.

System Modules

What the System Can Do

Dashboard

A central real-time portal where administrators and staff monitor all active incidents, student concerns, attendance flags, and system activity at a glance.

Emergency Alert

Students and staff can instantly trigger emergency notifications to school management, ensuring fast response to on-campus emergencies.

Facility Mapping

An interactive virtual diagram of the school premises that pinpoints the exact location of reported incidents or ongoing emergencies.

Incident Reporting

A structured reporting module for submitting, tracking, and managing safety events on school premises, complete with status updates and audit history.

Attendance Monitoring

Tracks semester-based student attendance records and flags patterns that may indicate welfare concerns or safety risks requiring counselor intervention.

Student Support Services

Coordinates and documents all student support activities handled by counselors and staff, ensuring no student concern goes unaddressed.

Personnel Tracking

Monitors the participation and task completion of administrators, teachers, counselors, and staff involved in safety and support activities.

Login / Authentication

Role-based secure access for all users — students, teachers, counselors, staff, and administrators — ensuring each user only accesses what they are authorized for.

Definition of Terms

Key Concepts & Terminology

Administrator (Admin)

An authorized user — a school official or appointed staff — with unrestricted access to the system's dashboard to monitor, manage, and respond to reported incidents and user accounts.

Dashboard

The main portal used by administrators and school staff to monitor, manage, and address reported incidents and student concerns.

Emergency Alert

A system feature that enables students or staff to notify school management of emergencies occurring on school premises.

Facility Mapping

A virtual diagram of the school premises integrated into the system to indicate the location of reported incidents or emergencies.

Incident Reporting

The process of submitting reports on safety issues or events occurring on school premises through the system.

Login / Authentication

The process by which users (students, teachers, staff, administrators) verify their identity — typically via username and password — to gain authorized access to the system.
